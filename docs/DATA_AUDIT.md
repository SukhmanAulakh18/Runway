## 3. Quirks, and what we do about each

| Quirk | Evidence | Our decision (in engine.py) |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | PKDD'99 Czech bank data, re-dated to 2020–2025, money relabelled CAD | Say so openly; use Canadian sources only to size shocks (EI cap) |
| Cash economy | 75% of money in is cash deposits; 76% of money out is cash withdrawals | Say 'money in / money out', not income/spending; EI share is an explicit slider |
| Thin labels | 51% of transactions have no purpose | Call unlabelled outflows 'purpose unknown'; never assume they're discretionary |
| No labelled bills | 1,225 accounts have median household bills = 0 | Flag it: a 'bills +X%' shock changes nothing in the standard formula |
| Negative buffer | 23 accounts have buffer ≤ 0 | Runway 0 before any shock; flagged |
| Stale accounts | 76 accounts have no activity in the final month(s) | Say 'no activity after YYYY-MM', never 'closed' |
| Opening month | an account's opening balance is a median 2% of its next 5 months' average (a normal 6-month trough ratio is 0.66), so windows that include the opening look like a near-zero dip | Trough score only with 12+ months of history |
| Pensioners | 740 accounts receive pension income | Pension is protected in a layoff (realistic runway); flagged |
| Minors | 57 account owners are under 18 | Flagged: job-loss shocks may not apply |
| District data | 2022 vs 2023 unemployment correlate 0.98; Czech regions | Not used to size Canadian shocks |
| District 69 | missing 2022 unemployment and crime | Not used by the app |
| Loan outcomes | defaults (B/D) have no default date | We don't predict *when*; we test signals measured before the loan |

**Summary:** all integrity checks pass.
